# Silver+ Operating Manual

## 1. Start here

**Silver Bank and Silver Care | Web and mobile | English | 20 October 2026**

This guide is for everyday members, with a short section for Community Partners
and administrators. Button names use the English interface. "Tap" also means
"click" when you are using a computer.

**Website:** https://silver-bank.s20181076-cfd.workers.dev

**Silver Bank** helps you request help, offer your time and participate in
community activities. **Silver Care** keeps your own wellbeing notes and selected
watch readings together. They use the same account, but a community task does
not automatically include your private health information.

### A simple first visit

1. Open the website or the installed Silver Bank app.
2. Choose **Sign In**. Sign in, register, or choose **Continue as guest**.
3. Keep your recovery code somewhere private and safe.
4. Open **Home** to explore community help, or **Care > Overview** for wellbeing.
5. Complete profile, agreements and verification before posting or accepting tasks.
6. Read and accept Care's separate privacy setup before saving health records.

### Choose the right mode

| Mode | What it is for | What to remember |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signed-in member | Real records, tasks and settings | Most account actions need internet. |
| Guest account | A real account without email/password | Records are real; keep the recovery code. Guests are not automatically verified or given credit. |
| Demo | Practising with sample information | Changes stay in memory and disappear when you exit or restart. Many real actions are deliberately unavailable. |
| Watch without internet | Receiving live Bluetooth readings in the installed app | No sign-in or cloud uploads. This is not an offline replacement for the full account. |

**Safety:** Silver Care is not emergency care, a diagnostic tool or continuous
medical monitoring. Contact local emergency services when urgent help is needed
(999 in Hong Kong). A check-in does not summon help.

### Find a topic

Sections 2-3: navigation and accounts. Sections 4-6: community requests,
completing tasks and programs. Sections 7-11: Care, watch, sharing and settings.
Section 12: partner/admin access. Section 13: troubleshooting and practice.

## 2. Find your way around

The main member navigation is the **bottom dock**, on both web and mobile.
Scroll within a page to reach forms and additional options.

| Destination | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | See available Time Credit, search nearby requests, choose **Give Time** or **Get Help**. |
| **Community** | Browse tasks, view your requests, find recommendations and open **My talents**. |
| **Care** | Open the menu of eight private wellbeing sections below. |
| **Time Bank** | Check available/reserved time and your transaction history. |
| **Profile** | Change personal details, language and skills; open verification or sign out. |

### The Care menu

Tap **Care**, then choose a destination. On a computer, keyboard users can open
the menu, use the arrow keys, and press Enter. Escape closes it.

| Care section | What you will find |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | A private check-in, recent records and shortcuts. |
| **My Health** | Saved records, watch graphs, editable notes and summary selection. |
| **AI chat** | Optional conversation, voice features and **Write a record**. |
| **Timeline** | Your personal observations and received device readings. |
| **My Watch** | Bluetooth controls, sensing options and separate saving permissions. |
| **Appointments** | Editable summaries, calendar export and selected-summary sharing. |
| **Family** | Private contacts and links to your device's calling app. |
| **Care settings** | Activity preferences, reminders, sharing access and data controls. |

The **bell** opens notifications. The **profile circle** opens your profile.
The logo returns to the introduction.

### What is remembered?

Safe navigation choices, applied task filters, watch options and some display
preferences are remembered separately for each account and device. Profile and
Care forms still need their **Save** button.

Unsaved form text is not guaranteed to survive navigation or a restart. The app
does not automatically repeat a booking, payment, check-in or sharing action.
On a shared computer, sign out when finished and do not leave recovery codes visible.

## 3. Accounts, recovery and verification

### Register as an individual

1. From **Sign In**, choose **New here? Create an account**.
2. Choose the individual/community-member option, not the organization option.
3. Enter the requested name, email, password, birth date, service area, phone
   number and preferred language. Use the country-code selector for your phone.
4. Read the agreements, tick the required acknowledgements, enter your name
   and draw your signature.
5. Submit the form. Save the recovery key shown during account creation.
6. Follow the profile/verification guidance before starting new community tasks.

The minimum participation age follows the community's current policy.
Registration alone does not complete identity or age verification.

### Continue as guest

1. Choose **Continue as guest** on the sign-in screen.
2. Read and confirm the explanation, then create the guest account.
3. Save the private recovery code and acknowledge that you have kept it.
4. Enter the app. Complete profile setup from the guest banner or **Profile**
   when you want to participate in community activities.

Your guest code grants account access. Do not send it to another member, include
it in a screenshot or use it in a task description. It cannot be retrieved if lost.

### Return to an existing account

- **Email account:** sign in with the same email and password.
- **Forgotten password:** choose **Forgot your password?** and use your saved
  recovery key. Do not expect an automatic password-reset email.
- **Guest account:** choose **Continue as guest**, then **Already have a guest
  recovery code?** Enter the original code rather than creating another guest.

Guest recovery invalidates earlier sessions. Reconnect devices or sign in again
where requested. Records belong to the account and backend, not merely the phone.
An account from an older hosting service may not exist on the current website.

### Complete verification

Open **Profile > Verification and organization staff** or the verification
notice. Follow the current instructions and contact verification staff for the
required human review. Do not post identity documents or document numbers in
ordinary chat. If feedback requests more information, correct and resubmit it.

**Expected result:** new task creation and acceptance become available only
after required profile, agreement and verification checks are satisfied.
Approval does not guarantee a particular Time Credit balance.

## 4. Post a request for help

**Route:** **Home > Get Help**, or **Community > Ask your community**.

### Before you begin

Use a real account, finish the participation requirements and check your available
Time Credit. A request reserves its estimated duration. For example, a 30-minute
request needs at least 30 minutes available; reserved time cannot fund another request.

### Fill in the request

1. **What do you need help with?** Give a short, specific title.
2. **Tell your helper what is involved:** explain the work and practical needs.
   Leave out passwords, recovery codes and unnecessary private health details.
3. Choose a **Category**.
4. Choose **In person** or **Remote**, and the appropriate service area.
5. Select a future **Requested date and time**.
6. Enter the **Estimated duration** in whole minutes.
7. Check the time reservation and balance shown below the form.
8. Tap **Post request**.

**Example:** "Help me practise video calls" / "Show me how to start and answer a
video call on my phone" / Remote / tomorrow afternoon / 30 minutes.

**Expected result:** the task detail page opens. Find it again in
**Community > Requested**. If you see an error instead, the task has not been
confirmed as posted; read the error and check your Requested list before retrying.

### Optional drafting help

Open **Describe the help you need**. If you agree to sending that description to
Groq, give permission and choose **Prepare my request**. Review every suggested
field. **Suggest a category** is optional too. Neither action posts the task.
You can complete the form manually without AI.

### If posting is blocked

- Verification message: complete the required review; a guest is not exempt.
- Insufficient balance: shorten the request only if realistic, earn time by
  helping, or check whether an eligible support program is available.
- Invalid date: choose a future date and check your device clock.
- No matching category/location: select an offered category and valid service area.

Care notes do not automatically become community requests. Post separately in
Silver Bank and choose carefully what to disclose.

## 5. Help someone and complete a task

### Find a suitable request

1. Open **Community > Give Time**.
2. Optionally open **Filter requests**, choose filters and tap **Apply filters**.
   The date filter is labelled **UTC**; account for your local time zone.
3. Open a task card. Read the description, area, time and expected credit.
4. Use the task's available conversation/contact controls to clarify the work.
   Sending a message does not accept the task.
5. Choose **Accept task** when ready. Participation checks still apply.
   Some partner tasks require a valid training badge first.

Accepted tasks appear under **Helping** for the helper and **Requested** for
the requester. **Recommended for you** provides suggestions, not a booking or
a promise of suitability.

### Agree, start, finish

1. Both participants open the task and choose **Agree to this date**.
2. Optionally choose **Add to calendar** and actually import/save the calendar file.
3. At the agreed time, when both people are ready, each chooses **Start now**.
   The timer begins only after both have started.
4. When the activity ends, one participant chooses **We've finished**.
5. The other checks the recorded time and chooses **Confirm completion**.
6. Check the completed status and the Time Bank entry. You may submit a review.

Accepting a task or agreeing to the date does not start its timer. A calendar
download alone is not a reminder until imported into a calendar.

### Change or cancel

The requester can use **Change date > Propose new date** before the task starts.
Both participants must confirm the new date again; update calendar reminders too.
For an open or accepted request, use **Cancel this request** and supply a reason.

If there is a disagreement during an eligible task stage, use
**Report a problem or disagree > Request administrator review**. Do not confirm
incorrect time just to clear the screen. Disputed credit remains held pending review.

### Read your Time Bank

**Available** is spendable time. **Reserved** is held for active requests, not
an additional balance. **Time contributed/received** summarize activity.
Open a task-linked transaction to inspect the task. Supported-time schemes,
where offered, have their own eligibility and review rules.

## 6. Talents, programs and training

### Tell the community what you can share

1. Open **Community > My talents**.
2. Describe your skills and experience.
3. Select activities you enjoy, meeting style, preferred days, comfortable duration
   and time zone.
4. Tap **Save my talents** and wait for the saved confirmation.
5. Choose **Find matching opportunities** to open recommendations.

You can update these preferences later. Recommendations do not guarantee
availability, eligibility, medical suitability or acceptance. Raw Care records
are not used to match your skills.

### Explore programs

Open **Community Programs & Training** from Home or Community, or
**Programs & Skill Badges** from Profile.

- Read the organizer, location, schedule, capacity and requirements.
- **Join program** registers interest/enrolment where allowed; it does not
  automatically complete the activity.
- For training, read the materials and modules. Use **Submit completion for review**
  when you have met the stated requirements.
- A Skill Badge depends on authorized review. It is community preparation,
  not a professional qualification; badges may expire or be revoked.
- Skill exchanges can offer sharer/learner roles. Follow the program's confirmation
  process and use the linked task where credit is involved.
- **Request supported time** is available only for applicable support programs.
  Explain eligibility and the time needed. Approval and budget availability are
  not guaranteed.

If no programs are listed, none are currently published for the view. Demo mode
does not publish programs, make real enrolments or issue credit.

## 7. Care check-ins and personal notes

**Care is for individual accounts.** Organization access does not grant access
to members' health records. Read and accept the separate Care privacy setup
before saving real information.

### Save a simple check-in

1. Open **Care > Overview**.
2. Choose **I am fine**, **I am resting**, **I feel unwell**, or
   **Please ask someone to call me**.
3. Read the explanation and choose **Confirm and save privately**.
4. Wait for the saved confirmation. Open **Timeline** to see the record.

**Important:** "Please ask someone to call me" records a wish. It sends no message,
makes no call and does not notify a caregiver. Contact someone directly when needed.

### Write a detailed note without AI

1. Open **Care > AI chat > Write a record**. You can also use **Record a concern**
   or **Add a record**, then choose **Write a record** on the conversation screen.
2. Enter when it happened and **What you experienced**.
3. Add optional details such as affected area, onset, duration or changes.
4. Choose **Review my record**.
5. Check the details, then **Confirm and save privately**.

The manual-record form also offers **Daily check-in** for optional wellbeing,
energy, movement, sleep and discomfort notes.

### Review, edit or delete

Open **My Health** or **Timeline**. Find a record, choose **Edit record**, change
the information and complete the review/save steps. To remove one, open
**Delete record** and confirm.

Previously shared summaries are separate snapshots; editing or deleting a source
note does not silently rewrite a copy already shared or downloaded.

### Understand the graphs

**My Health** distinguishes information you reported from device readings.
Use **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days**, the exact-value table and
**Refresh readings**. A gap is missing data, not a zero or a medical conclusion.
Step values are watch-counter observations and are not necessarily daily totals.
This view shows recent records/readings; older on-phone watch history is separate.

## 8. Optional AI conversation and voice

**Route:** **Care > AI chat**.

### Start a conversation

1. Read **Before we start**. Messages are sent to Groq for AI replies.
2. Decide whether **Save my physical concerns to My Health** should be enabled.
3. Choose **Agree and start** only if you accept that processing.
4. Type in **Message Silver Care**, review the text and send it.

You can use **Write a record** instead without sending a conversation to AI.
AI can be mistaken and does not diagnose, prescribe or provide emergency monitoring.

### Speak instead of typing

1. Use the large microphone button if your platform supports it.
2. Read the voice notice, choose **Enable microphone**, and grant the browser/
   device permission when prompted.
3. Speak, stop recording and review the transcript.
4. Send the text yourself.

Some browsers process speech remotely. Audio is not stored by Silver Care.
If voice input is unavailable, type or use the device keyboard's dictation.

Open **Conversation options** to choose the available voice language, enable or
disable **Read replies aloud**, and change automatic-note saving. Use
**Stop spoken reply** to stop playback. Voice support varies by browser and device.

### What is saved?

The current conversation is not a durable chat archive. With the optional
automatic-note setting, eligible quotations about physical concerns can become
private notes. Review those notes in **My Health** and correct or delete them.
AI selection is not a medical assessment; emotional-only conversation should not
be treated as a physical symptom record.

If a reply arrives but its note fails to save, the app reports the failure.
Use **Write a record** to keep the information. If AI is unavailable or a usage
limit is reached, manual records remain available.

## 9. Connect and use your watch

**Route:** installed mobile app, **Care > My Watch**.

### Connect PineTime

1. Keep the watch powered on, nearby and adequately charged.
2. Enable Bluetooth on the phone and grant the app's Bluetooth permission.
3. Enable **Allow watch readings and automatic reconnection on this device**.
4. Tap **Connect watch**, choose the intended watch and complete any pairing prompt.
5. Wait for **Connected** and check each reading's received time.

The ordinary website shows **Installed mobile app required** for this receiver.
Creating a private-records adapter is a developer integration step, not Bluetooth
pairing. Do not share adapter keys or paste them into public messages.

### Enable periodic sensing

Choose **Automatically measure heart rate every 15 minutes** only on compatible
firmware. Keep the app open unless the separate supported iPhone background option
is enabled. Each session allows warm-up, then ten seconds of usable estimates,
with a 60-second overall limit. Failed attempts and gaps are possible.

If offered, **Continue sensing when my iPhone locks and keep private on-phone
history** requires compatible iPhone integration and Mastro 0.1.13 or newer.
Wait until the app reports background sensing is armed before locking it.
Physical locked-phone reliability is still under validation; do not rely on it
for safety-critical monitoring.

### Receive, store locally and upload are different

| Action | Internet needed? | Where readings go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receive Bluetooth | No | Live app session; watch and phone must remain connected. |
| Supported on-phone history | No after setup | Private, account/backend-scoped storage on that phone. |
| Save to private account | Yes for synchronization | The selected categories in the online account. |

For cloud saving, finish Care setup, select **Save selected watch readings to my
private Silver Bank account**, choose categories and tap **Start saving**.
Wait for **Saved to your private account**. **Waiting to sync** is not confirmation
of a completed upload. Use **Retry sync** after fixing a connection error.

**Stop saving** disables automatic uploads but does not delete saved records.
**Disconnect** stops automatic reception/reconnection. Local and online copies
have separate deletion controls. Removing the app can lose local-only history.

### Offline and power limits

From sign-in, **Use watch without internet** opens live-only watch mode without
account uploads. It is not full offline access to tasks or health records.
A dark watch display can still work; a fully powered-off watch cannot transmit.
Force-quitting, system termination, radio loss or distance can interrupt reception.
Reopen the app and check status and timestamps before trusting a displayed value.

## 10. Prepare appointment notes and share carefully

### Build an editable summary

1. Open **Care > My Health**.
2. Tick **Include in summary** for the notes you want, and **Include** for selected
   device readings.
3. Choose **Prepare selected information**.
4. Review the title and text. Remove anything unnecessary and add your questions.
5. Optionally enter an appointment date/time.
6. Choose **Confirm and save summary**.

Alternatively, open **Appointments > Write appointment notes** to start with your
own text. This organizes information; it does not book a healthcare appointment
or generate a clinical interpretation.

### Bring a copy

Open the saved summary and choose **Download selected summary** for a text file.
Store it privately. If a date was entered, **Add calendar reminder** downloads
a calendar event; import it and check the calendar's reminder permissions.
Download/import behavior can vary between the browser and installed app.

### Share one selected summary

1. Open **Share this summary with someone** on the saved summary.
2. Enter the recipient's existing individual Silver+ account email.
3. Choose expiry: **1, 7 or 30 days**.
4. Read the summary, confirm authorization and choose **Share selected summary**.
5. The recipient opens **Care > Appointments > Shared with me**.

This shares a frozen snapshot, not your whole account or future edits. A family
contact entry is not an account invitation. Do not assume an email notification
was sent.

Manage and revoke online access in **Care settings**. Downloaded copies cannot
be recalled. Deleting a summary removes its online sharing access, not copies
someone has already saved elsewhere.

## 11. Family, privacy and remembered options

### Add someone you may want to call

1. Open **Care > Family > Add contact**.
2. Enter a name, optional relationship and international phone number.
3. Choose **Save contact**.
4. Use **Call** when you want to open the device's calling app.

Calling requires a compatible calling app or telephone service. It is not an
automatic caregiver call. Adding a person never grants health-record access.
Use **Edit** or **Remove > Confirm removal** to update your private contact list.

### Care preferences

Open **Care > Care settings**.

- Optional broad preferences can help order Bank recommendations: lower-intensity,
  shorter, remote or nearby activities.
- Enabling that connection does not share symptoms, check-ins or raw readings,
  restrict task choice, or create a booking.
- Choose available in-app confirmations and daily reminder settings, then save.
- Import any offered calendar reminder if you want a calendar notification.
  The app does not promise background push, SMS or emergency alerts.
- Review shared-summary access and revoke access you no longer want to allow.
- Read the cloud-deletion confirmation carefully before using it. Download any
  selected summaries you want to keep from Appointments first. Online Care erasure
  and local watch-history deletion are separate operations.

### Profile and device preferences

Open **Profile** to change language, service area, phone details, skills and
preferred categories. Use **Save profile** and wait for confirmation.
Some Care/partner text remains English even when a different interface language
is selected; do not expect user-written text or agreements to be translated.

**Reset saved options** clears remembered options on this device and stops
automatic watch activity. It does not delete server-side account records.
It is different from deleting Care data or uninstalling the app.

### Before signing out or changing phones

Keep your recovery key. If you want online copies of local watch readings, enable
the selected cloud-saving categories and confirm synchronization before changing
phones. Local-only history does not transfer automatically. A different phone
needs its own Bluetooth pairing and consent. Sign out from Profile;
do not create a new guest merely because the original session expired.

## 12. Community Partners and administrators

These are role-specific areas. Ordinary members should not create an organization
account just to ask a personal question or use Care.

### Community Partner access

Choose the NGO/Community Partner registration option and supply the requested
organization and representative details. Verification staff review the organization
and authority to act for it. Approval must remain current.

Authorized users open **Community Partner Dashboard** from Profile. The partner
dock uses **Overview**, **Needs**, **Programs**, **Training** and **Profile**.
Additional workspace sections include community tasks, time support, participants
and insights, subject to role permissions.

Typical workflow:

1. Select the correct authorized organization.
2. Open **Programs** or **Training** and create the relevant draft.
3. Enter title, description, location, schedule, duration, capacity and requirements.
4. For training, supply materials, modules, completion requirements and badge details.
5. Review the saved draft and publish only when ready.
6. Review participant progress and take permitted completion/badge actions.

Owners manage organization details and staff access. Program/training managers
have different responsibilities; some staff have view/review-communication access
only. If an action is missing or denied, check role and current verification
rather than using another person's credentials.

Partner accounts do not receive private Care access or an automatic personal
starter grant. Programs and funding actions need the relevant permissions and
available budget. Organization verification is not a guarantee of every service.

### Administrators

Authorized administrators use **Profile > Community administration** for the
available community controls and verification administration for reviews.
Record accurate reasons for adjustments, disputes and decisions. Examine original
evidence through the approved review process; do not approve identity checks just
to make a task button work.

Administrator status does not grant blanket access to private Care records.
Never use a real member account as a test fixture.

## 13. Troubleshooting and a safe practice route

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Cannot sign in | Check backend/site, account type and credentials. A guest uses its recovery code, not an email password. |
| Verification required | Open Profile's verification section and follow the review instructions. |
| Post request is disabled | Check required fields, future time and available credit. |
| No tasks/programs | Broaden filters, change the task tab, or check again later. Demo has no real bookings. |
| Start now is missing | Both people must agree to the date; starting is available at the scheduled time. |
| Waiting for confirmation | The other participant must confirm the recorded completion. |
| A record changed | Reopen the latest version and review it before saving again. |
| Microphone unavailable | Check permission/platform support; use text or keyboard dictation. |
| AI error or limit | Do not keep resending private text; use Write a record and try later. |
| Watch connection lost | Power on the watch, enable Bluetooth, move it nearby and reopen the app. |
| Old heart-rate value | Check received time and sensing status; an old value is not a fresh measurement. |
| Waiting to sync / Sync failed | Restore internet; confirm account/Care consent and selected categories; use Retry sync. |
| Calendar reminder absent | Import/save the event and check calendar notification settings. |
| Options not restored | Check the same device/account/backend; look for a storage error. Unsaved forms are not remembered. |
| A page cannot load | Reload once or return to sign-in. Keep the recovery code; do not clear data or uninstall as the first step. |

### Practise without real transactions

1. Sign In > **Try demo without signing in**.
2. Open Home, Community, Time Bank and Profile.
3. Open Programs; an empty program list is normal in the demo.
4. Care > Overview: choose **I am resting** and save the sample check-in.
5. Open Timeline to find it. Practise editing it and review the result.
6. My Health: inspect the labelled sample graphs and exact values.
7. Visit My Watch, Appointments, Family and Care settings without granting real
   permissions or expecting real bookings, calls, AI processing or sharing.
8. Choose **Exit demo**. All sample changes are discarded.

### Report a problem usefully

Record the screen name, exact button sequence, error text, approximate time,
phone/browser version, internet state and whether the account is guest, member
or demo. Include app/firmware versions for watch issues.

Never include passwords, recovery keys, adapter keys, identity documents or
private health notes in a public report. Redact sensitive details from screenshots.

**Release note:** this manual describes the current source behavior and tested
fixes. Installed apps and the live site only gain a fix after the corresponding
release. See the separate validation report for what was checked and what remains
unverified; no test run can establish that every possible error is absent.
